## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-07

First published version. **Beta** — a working Scratch player with a real
per-frame Bevy scheduler and a broad slice of the block set, but with real,
documented gaps (see [README.md](README.md#roadmap--known-gaps)) rather than
full parity with `scratch-vm`.
